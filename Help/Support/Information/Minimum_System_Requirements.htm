Minimum System Requirements
  • Linux operating system
  • Pentium® 233Mhz MMX® processor with 8 MB Video Card
     or Pentium II 266Mhz processor with 4 MB Video Card
     or AMD® 350Mhz K6®-2 processor with 4 MB Video Card
  • 64 MB RAM
  • 3D Hardware Accelerator with full OpenGL® support*
  • OSS compatible sound card
  • XFree86 SVGA 3.3.5 server
  • 25 MB of uncompressed hard disk space for game files (Minimum Install)
  • Quad-speed CD-ROM drive (600 K/sec. sustained transfer rate)
  • Multiplayer Requirements
    - Internet and LAN play supported
    - Internet play requires a 28.8 Kbps (or faster) modem

Quake III Arena is optimized for Intel® and AMD® processors.   Cyrix processors are not supported by Loki Customer Support. 

* See the README on disk for 3D card specific requirements. A 100% full OpenGL compliant 3D video card is required.  Quake III Arena uses OpenGL to support 3D hardware acceleration.  Quake III Arena has been tested on many but not all of the major cards incorporating the chipsets listed below.  Some cards (using some of the chipsets listed below) may require reference drivers, which are included with this product.  For the most recent list of cards and drivers supported, please visit the support section at www.lokigames.com.
